## v2.5.6

Restores V1's spoofer download speed. Jobs that were taking 5+ minutes on a handful of private/copylocked assets now finish in seconds.

### Fixed

- **Multi-minute stalls on jobs containing private assets.** A regression compared to V1 caused the download loop to try ~80 candidate URLs per private asset (up from ~4), each getting three 403 responses with sleeps between them. On a small job with two private assets that could burn 6+ minutes of wall time behind the shared rate limiter. Aligned four things back to V1's algorithm:
  - Discovery (usage + social-graph walks) now only runs when the caller didn't supply a place ID, matching V1. Was running unconditionally, which is what bloated the candidate URL list.
  - Removed the retry-on-403 loop on the asset delivery endpoint. A 403 there is terminal for that URL/cookie combo — retrying it just cost 3s per dead URL. Falls straight through to the next candidate now.
  - Social-graph graph queries fan out fully in parallel again (was throttled to 3 concurrent), which cut first-time-per-creator discovery from ~10s back to ~1s.
  - Per-URL retry budget tightened to 3 attempts / 30s timeout (was 10 / 45s).
- **Long download loops no longer look hung.** Added a heartbeat log every 10 candidate URLs (`"Still trying asset X: candidate N/total."`) and a bail-out after 5 consecutive permanent failures on a single asset, so a stuck asset can't hold the shared rate limiter for a whole job.
